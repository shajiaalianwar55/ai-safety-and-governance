# AI Safety & Governance

An open learning and research initiative at GIK Institute, exploring technical AI safety, alignment, evaluation and frontier AI governance.

## About

This initiative aims to introduce undergraduate students to AI safety research through structured learning, interactive discussions and reproducible technical experiments.

Our first cohort follows a four-week curriculum, progressing from foundational concepts to a collaborative research project.

## Curriculum

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations of AI Safety and Project Directions |
| 2 | AI Safety Evaluations and Benchmarking |
| 3 | Safety Monitoring, Governance and Experimental Design |
| 4 | Research Project, Analysis and Publication |

## Research Project

Our proposed research investigates the robustness of AI safety evaluations to changes in experimental design, particularly:

- Prompt sensitivity
- LLM judge sensitivity
- Consistency across target models

We intend to use Inspect AI and an existing safety benchmark to conduct controlled, reproducible experiments.

## Public Resources

This repository will be updated incrementally with:

- Lecture slides and notes
- Recommended readings

- Research methodology
- Experimental code and results
- Technical research write-ups

## Research Standards

We prioritise factual accuracy, primary sources, reproducibility, transparent methodology and honest reporting of limitations.
